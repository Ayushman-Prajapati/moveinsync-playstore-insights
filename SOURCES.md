# Sources and evidence notes

Research snapshot prepared on 12 October 2026. Public pages may change; the rating and review counts below are time-stamped observations, not permanent facts.

1. Official Google Play listing — MoveInSync (`com.moveinsync.ets`):
   https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.moveinsync.ets
   Observed listing: 4.3 stars, approximately 114K reviews; page metadata reported updated 30 August 2026 in the captured result.
   The listing excerpt included public reviews dated 11 June, 18 June, 29 June, 1 July and 7 July 2026. See `data/reviews_seed.csv`.

2. Chrome-Stats review summary:
   https://chrome-stats.com/d/com.moveinsync.ets/reviews
   Captured page reported 114,542 total ratings, 4.32 overall, a recent written-review average of 4.10, and a displayed distribution for a 1,146-review recent sample: 25% one-star. This is a third-party sample and is not the distribution of every Play Store rating. Treat it as a directional signal and verify independently before operational use.

3. MoveInSync app overview on Google Play:
   https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_IN&id=com.moveinsync.ets
   Describes the app's employee commute, scheduling, tracking and workplace functions.

## Evidence boundaries

- This repository intentionally does not claim to have collected all public reviews.
- The included seed CSV has five examples, not a statistically representative sample.
- Review ratings are blank in the seed where the source excerpt did not expose them.
- User text is paraphrased where indicated in the `notes` column; verify against the source URL before quoting verbatim.
- Keyword classification is an explainable baseline. It may miss synonyms, multi-language reviews, sarcasm and context. It is not a sentiment model.
- Public review sentiment is not a substitute for internal operational telemetry or controlled evaluation.
